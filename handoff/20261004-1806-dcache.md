WHAT: Adds the 68030 data cache on the data read port. 16 lines of four long words, valid per long word, tag A31:8 and FC2:0. Only an aligned long-word read can hit or fill. A miss passes BUS_RDY straight through. The hit strobe is a register. Component declared in wf68k30L_pkg.vhd. New file cpu030/wf68k30L_dcache.vhd, wired in wf68k30L_top.vhd, added to the build lists and run_rtl.sh. Does not touch the instruction cache or the diag overlay. Cable 417 only. Do not flash. Do not reflash TOS.
